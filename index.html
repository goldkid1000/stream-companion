Go-Live Checklist

    Settings Library

    Verified cards are your real, tested settings on the HP Victus (Radeon 660M iGPU + RX 6550M). Starting-point cards are untested suggestions to tune — never verified values.

    OBS — Stream output

    • Output resolution: 1280x720 for now
    • Encoder: AMD HW H.264, CBR 6000 (drop to 4500 if upload < 8 Mbps)
    • Keyframe interval: 2s · Rate control preset: Balanced · Profile: High
    • NEVER run the Auto-Configuration Wizard — it switches OBS to Simple mode and wipes VOD Track 2 + recording quality.
    • Process Priority: Above Normal · Run OBS as admin
    • OBS Preview: OFF when lagging

    OBS — Recording

    • Recording encoder: H.264 (not HEVC)
    • Format: MKV + auto-remux to MP4 · Quality: CQP 18-20
    • VOD audio: Track 2 with no music (keeps VODs safe from muted audio)

    OBS — Audio

    • Sample rate: 48kHz · Mic level: ~80% (raw approx -20 dB)
    • Filter chain, in order: RNNoise → Noise Gate (-45/-50) → Gain +3..+6 → Compressor 3:1 @ -18 → Limiter -3

    OBS — Black overlay fix (660M)

    • Uncheck “Enable Browser Source Hardware Acceleration” in OBS Settings → Advanced, then do a full OBS restart. This fixes black browser overlays when OBS runs on the 660M.

    PC — Dual-GPU assignments

    • OBS: Power saving (660M) · Games: High performance (RX 6550M)
    • AMD Adrenalin on the 6550M: AFMF OFF + RSR OFF
    • AMD Adrenalin on the 660M: everything OFF

    When it lags — read OBS Stats

    • Rendering lag = GPU overloaded (lower game settings / output resolution). Encoding lag = encoder overloaded (lower bitrate or switch encoder). Network/dropped frames = connection (lower bitrate toward 4500).

              Growth Playbook

              📅 Fixed schedule

              Pick 2–3 fixed days/times and fill the Schedule tab. Consistency feeds the Affiliate day + minute gates and gives viewers a reason to come back.

              🎬 One TikTok clip per stream

              Your TikTok (@burry2006) is the funnel. Cut one clip from every stream and post it — clips bring people to Twitch, not the other way around.

              🎙️ Narrate the farm

              Talk even to an empty chat: call out what you're farming, why, and what the build does. It also makes your avatar move — silence looks dead on stream.

              ⚔️ Raid small Warframe streamers

              End streams by raiding a similar small Warframe streamer. They notice, their chat notices, and some follow back.

              🧹 Delete the stale widget

              Remove the 62/70 goals widget below your player — it contradicts your real 60 followers and confuses new viewers.

              📊 Live Goals bar in-video

              Put the live StreamElements Goals bar in your video (not a static number) so viewers see real progress toward 100.

              Empty-Chat Prompts

              Chat quiet? Say one of these out loud. Talking keeps the stream alive — and moves your avatar.

              🌾 Farm callouts

              • “Right now I'm farming [item] because I need it for [build/weapon].”
              • “This is run number [X] — let's see if the drop finally cooperates.”
              • “Here's the fastest route I use for this farm, step by step.”
              • “If you're farming this too, the one mistake to avoid is…”

              💬 Questions for viewers

              • “What's your main right now — and what frame should I try next?”
              • “What are you farming this week? Anyone else stuck on the same drop?”
              • “New players in chat: what's the one thing you wish someone explained sooner?”
              • “Chat, settle it: which is the better starter farm — relics or credits?”

              🛠️ Talk about the build

              • “Here's my build and why each mod is there — ask me about any slot.”
              • “This weapon feels completely different with this one mod swapped.”
              • “I'm testing this build today — tell me if it looks off or cracked.”
              • “The last change I made to this loadout was [X], and here's what it fixed.”
